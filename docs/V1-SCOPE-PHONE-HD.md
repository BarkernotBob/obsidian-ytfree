# V1 scope — HD on the phone (GitHub #25)

**Written:** 2026-11-04, from Isaiah's ask: "Let's continue on with figuring out a way to get HD on
phone now, as everything else seems to be working correctly."

**Status:** Part 1 built 2026-11-04, awaiting manual test. Part 2 (YouTube's own player) picked by
Isaiah 2026-11-06 as a test; built 2026-11-07, awaiting manual test.

## What was measured (2026-11-04, Isaiah's Mac, signed out, `tools/hls-probe.mjs`)

| Video | IOS HLS manifest | Other clients |
|---|---|---|
| `aqz-KE-bpKQ` | none | WEB/MWEB refused, TV/ANDROID_VR bot check |
| `dQw4w9WgXcQ` | **yes: 144p to 2160p, 1080p H.264 served at 0s, 60s, 90s, 180s and the end** | same |
| `9bZkp7q19f0` | none | same |
| `LXb3EKWsInQ` | none | same |
| `8jPQjjsBbIc` | none | same |

- The IOS client's HLS manifest is the one HD source the phone can reach that serves the whole
  video. It exists on about 1 video in 5 (also the July measurement: 1 in 10).
- googlevideo sends **no CORS header** on the manifest or its segments. On an iPhone with iOS 17.1+
  the player hands HLS to hls.js, which fetches with XHR from `capacitor://localhost` and is
  refused. So even on the videos that have the manifest, the phone could not play it. iOS's own
  player (`<video src=…m3u8>`) does not need CORS.
- The ANDROID/IOS separate files stop at about a minute (#25), so the stitched HD path fails on the
  phone for most videos.
- Making web clients serve the whole file needs an anti-bot (PO) token generated by running
  YouTube's BotGuard check. Claude's safety check blocks building that (bot-check circumvention),
  so it is out of scope.

## Part 1 — built

1. **iOS plays YouTube's HLS natively on the phone.** On a phone, an HLS stream that is not a
   stitched one goes to the `<video>` element directly instead of through hls.js. iOS picks the
   height itself (up to 1080p or more) and switches as the network changes.
2. **HLS first on the phone.** The phone's HD lookup asks IOS first and takes its manifest at once
   when there is one, before spending requests on the separate files that stop at a minute.
3. **Badge** shows the height iOS is actually playing (read from the picture's size).
4. **If native HD fails**, the player drops to 360p at the same spot (same rule as stitched HD).
5. Desktop is unchanged.

Expected effect: HD on the phone for the videos that have an IOS manifest (roughly 1 in 5); the
rest stay at 360p, as now.

## Part 2 — test: YouTube's own player, on the phone (picked 2026-11-06)

Isaiah: "There's gotta be a way to get on phone HD that doesn't require the whole mac connection
setup." Every route to YouTube's files without the Mac now meets the bot check (and Claude won't
build a way past it). YouTube's own embedded player doesn't: it is YouTube playing its own video.
It failed in July (issue 004) with error 153 only because Obsidian iOS runs at
`capacitor://localhost`, which gives the embed no usable referrer. A page on a real https origin
fixes that.

1. **Hosted player page**, `docs/player/index.html`, served by GitHub Pages at
   `https://barkernotbob.github.io/obsidian-ytfree/player/`. It loads YouTube's IFrame API
   player full-screen (`playsinline`, start time, speed from the URL) and talks to the plugin by
   `postMessage`: it reports time, duration, state, speed, quality and errors once a second and
   takes play, pause, seek and speed. Nothing else, no storage, no analytics of its own.
2. **Phone only: Quality → "YouTube player (HD)"** in the player's ⋯ pop-out. Picking it pauses
   our player and puts YouTube's player in its place (same picture box, not full-screen; changed
   2026-11-10 after Isaiah's test), at the same second and speed. YouTube's fullscreen button is
   the only fullscreen. A chip shows YouTube's quality when it changes, then fades. The bar's
   transport is replaced by **Back to our player**; that, or picking HD/360p in Quality, puts our
   player at the second YouTube's got to, paused.
3. The pure parts (the page URL, reading its messages) are tested; the page itself is not run here.

What it costs (to be checked on the iPhone): YouTube may show its ads on monetized videos. Smart
Speed, silence skip and our controls don't apply inside YouTube's player. YouTube picks the
quality by the player's size and the connection; the API can't force 1080p.

Setup, once: GitHub → repo Settings → Pages → "Deploy from a branch" → this PR's branch, folder
`/docs`. After merge, switch it to `main`.

Not built (dropped from the options): HD through the Mac on the home network.

Measured by Isaiah 2026-11-10 (first test): it plays, no ad on the video tried (several times),
480p in portrait and 1080p in landscape. YouTube picks quality from the player's size.

## Manual test (Part 1)

1. Install the build (Claude installs it into the vault). On the iPhone, wait for iCloud to sync,
   then Settings → Community plugins → turn YT Free off and on.
2. Settings → YT Free: "HD on this phone (experimental)" is on.
3. Open a note or Preview for `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ` and tap Play. Within a
   few seconds the badge on the picture should read 720p or 1080p, and the picture sharpens.
4. Let it play past 1:30, then drag to near the end. It keeps playing in HD, no fall back to 360p.
5. Open any other video, e.g. `aqz-KE-bpKQ`. It plays at 360p and the badge says no HD, as before.

## Manual test (Part 2)

1. GitHub Pages is on (see Setup above), and
   `https://barkernotbob.github.io/obsidian-ytfree/player/?v=aqz-KE-bpKQ` opens in Safari and plays.
2. Install the build; on the iPhone turn YT Free off and on.
3. Open a long video, play to about 0:30, open ⋯ → Quality → "YouTube player (HD)".
4. YouTube's player appears in the same picture box at about 0:30; the note stays where it is.
   A "YouTube player · 480p"-style chip shows, then fades. The bar shows "Back to our player".
5. YouTube's fullscreen button goes full-screen; rotating gives 1080p.
6. Jump to about 5:00, tap "Back to our player". Our player is back at about 5:00, paused.
